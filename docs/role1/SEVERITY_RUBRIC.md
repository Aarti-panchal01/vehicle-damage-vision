# Severity rubric used for the draft labels

The team plan fixes the three words (minor, moderate, severe) but not what they mean. This rubric is what the draft labels follow. It is written so that Role 2's repair-or-replace rules line up with it: minor and moderate damage is usually repairable, severe usually means replace.

| Severity | Body panels (dent, scratch, crack) | Lamps and glass | Tyres |
|---|---|---|---|
| **minor** | Cosmetic. Scratch that does not go through the paint, or a small dent (under about 5 cm) with the paint unbroken. Polish or paintless dent repair. | Light scuff on the lens, no crack. | Low pressure, tyre still holds shape. |
| **moderate** | Clearly visible. Dent about 5 to 30 cm, or paint broken through to primer or plastic, or a crack in a plastic part with no piece missing. Panel can be repaired and repainted. | Lens cracked but the lamp is still in place; a windshield chip or a crack that spreads from one impact point. | Flat on the rim, tyre itself intact (puncture repair). |
| **severe** | Panel torn, crushed, folded or with pieces missing, or deformed over most of its area. Usually replaced. | Lens shattered or missing, housing broken, or glass shattered across a large area or holed. | Tyre off the bead, torn, or wheel/rim bent. |

When two damages hit the same part, each gets its own severity. Contract A keeps both entries; the merged per-part severity used in scoring is the worst one.
